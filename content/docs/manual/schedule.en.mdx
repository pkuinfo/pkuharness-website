The Schedule tab brings together your timetable, to-dos, reminders and scheduled tasks. This page
explains how to add and change them, and how to make sure reminders arrive on time. You can also
ask the assistant in chat to create any of them, for example "Remind me to hand in my homework at 8
tomorrow morning".

The tab has two views at the top: **Agenda** and **Timetable**. Once a semester is set, the title
shows the current teaching week.

## Agenda

The Agenda view shows one day.

1. Pick a day in the week strip at the top. Days with classes are marked.
2. The three buttons below, **To-do**, **Reminder** and **Class**, each create a new item.
3. The page lists that day's classes (**Today's classes** for today), your **To-do** list, and
   **Reminders and scheduled tasks**.

Tap any item to open its edit page. The edit page has a **Delete** button at the bottom.

## Timetable

The Timetable view shows the whole week's classes. Use the arrows to move to the previous or next
week.

### Set the semester

Teaching weeks, odd/even-week classes and class reminders all depend on the semester. The academic
calendar syncs on its own once you sign in to your campus account. You can also set it yourself.

1. Under **Timetable settings** below the timetable, tap **Semester**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **First day of week 1** and **Teaching weeks**.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Sync from the university

This comes from the **Official timetable** plugin and needs your campus sign-in.

1. Under **Timetable settings**, tap **Sync timetable from the university**.
2. Tap **Sync timetable**.
3. If the page says **Verification needed**, choose **Authenticator** or **SMS**. For SMS, tap
   **Send SMS code** first. Enter the code and tap **Verify and sync**. If the app is your
   authenticator, the code is filled in for you.

Syncing replaces the classes from the last sync. Classes you added yourself stay as they are. Tap
**Sync again** later to update.

### Add a class

1. Tap **Add class** under **Timetable settings**, or **Class** at the top of the Agenda view.
2. Fill in **Class name**, **Day**, **Starts** and **Ends**, and optionally **Room** and
   **Teacher**.
3. In **Weeks**, enter the weeks the class meets, for example `1-16`, `1-15 odd`, `2-16 even` or
   `1,3,5`. Leave it empty for every week.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Class reminders

At the bottom of the Timetable view, under **Class reminders (in advance)**, choose **Off**,
**5 min**, **10 min**, **15 min** or **30 min**. Every class in your timetable then reminds you
that long before it starts.

## To-dos

Type in the box at the bottom of the **To-do** list and press return to add a to-do. For more
fields, tap **More options** next to the box, or the **To-do** button at the top.

The to-do edit page has these fields.

- **To-do** and **Notes**.
- **Due**.
- **Reminder**: **None**, **When due**, **15 min before** or **1 h before**. You need a due time to
  get a reminder. A to-do the assistant created may have another reminder time, shown as
  **Custom**.
- **Important**: flags the to-do and lists it under **Today** even without a due date.
- **Repeat**: **Never**, **Daily** or **Weekly**. Completing a repeating to-do creates the next
  one, and its reminder moves along.

To-dos are grouped as **Overdue**, **Today**, **Later** and **No date**. Check the box on the left to
complete one. Completed to-dos are folded at the bottom of the list; tap **Show completed** to see
them.

## Reminders

Tap **Reminder** at the top of the Agenda view to create a reminder.

1. Fill in **Title** and, optionally, **Message**. The message appears in the notification.
2. Choose **Once** or **Repeat**.
3. For **Once**, set the date and time.
4. For **Repeat**, set the repeat rule (see the next section).
5. Tap **Save**.

Each reminder in the list has a switch. Turning it off pauses the reminder without deleting it. The
list shows when each reminder fires next; one that has ended shows **Ended** and how many times it
ran.

### Repeat options

A repeating reminder uses one of four units: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Hourly** or **Minutes**.

- **Interval**: the number after **Every**, such as every 3 days or every 2 weeks.
- For **Weekly**, choose the days of the week.
- For **Daily** and **Weekly**, you can set several times of day; tap **Add time** to add one.
- For **Hourly** and **Minutes**, set the moment it starts **From**.

**Ends** has three choices.

| Choice | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Never ends | It doesn't stop by itself |
| After | It stops after a set number of times, for example 16 |
| On date | It runs up to and including that day |

For example, "every 3 days, 16 times" is: **Daily**, interval 3, **Ends** set to **After**, and 16.

## Scheduled tasks

Besides reminders, the assistant can create two kinds of scheduled task in chat. They appear in the
**Reminders and scheduled tasks** list as well.

A **scheduled check** looks up a value on a schedule and notifies you when a condition is met, for
example "Check my balance every morning at 8 and remind me if it's under ¥100".

- A check runs at most once every 15 minutes.
- It can only use read-only tools, so it never changes anything.
- Its edit page shows the condition, **Recent runs** and a **Check now** button. The AI set up the
  condition; to change it, tell the assistant in chat.
- After 10 failures in a row, the check pauses itself and notifies you. Once the cause is fixed,
  turn its switch back on in the list.

An **AI task** has the assistant do a task at set times in a fresh session, for example summarizing
the notices once a day.

- The assistant can only use read-only tools for it, and stays quiet when there's nothing worth
  telling you.
- It runs at most once an hour, and at most 10 can be on at the same time.
- Tools set to **Ask first** can't be used by scheduled tasks.
- Its edit page has a **Run now** button.

At most 50 reminders and scheduled tasks can be on at the same time.

## On-time delivery

Reminders and scheduled checks run on your phone and are woken by the system alarm, with no server
involved. To have them arrive on time, the app needs these permissions.

1. Open **Settings → Reminders and alerts**.
2. **Notifications**: tap **Allow**. If they are blocked, tap **Settings** and turn them on in
   Android's settings.
3. **Exact alarms**: tap **Turn on** and allow PKU harness in Android's "Alarms & reminders"
   setting. Without it, reminders may arrive a few minutes late.
4. **Unrestricted battery**: tap **Turn on**, then **Allow** in the system dialog. This lets
   scheduled checks run on time in the background.

The same page shows whether **Background checks** are available, the number of
**Scheduled notifications**, and a **Send a test reminder** button. The test reminder arrives in 10
seconds; go to the home screen or lock your phone to see it.

If notifications or exact alarms are missing, the Schedule tab shows a banner at the top. Tap
**Turn on** or **Open settings** to fix it.

In Android's notification settings, PKU harness has three categories: **Reminders** (schedule,
to-do and timed reminders), **Check results** (alerts from background checks) and
**Background checks** (shown briefly while a check runs).

If the phone was off or Android held the app back, so that a scheduled check or AI task would run
more than 2 hours after its time, that run is recorded as **Missed (too late)** and sends no
notification.
