This manual describes version 1.0.20 of the PKU Harness Android app. Each page covers one part of
the app. Button and menu names in the steps are the ones the app shows.

PKU Harness is an Android app that helps you with campus services. You sign in once with your PKU
student ID and password and connect an AI model service. You can then ask the assistant in chat to
check your Campus Card balance, sync your timetable, set reminders and more. Each feature that
connects to a campus service is provided by a plugin.

If you are new, start with [AI provider](/en/docs/manual/ai-model): the assistant can't do anything
until you connect a model service, and that page shows how to get a key step by step.

## The four tabs

The app has four tabs at the bottom.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Conversations with the assistant, which looks things up and acts for you |
| **Dashboard** | Widgets that show numbers such as balances and the teaching week |
| **Schedule** | Timetable, to-dos, reminders and scheduled tasks |
| **Settings** | Account, AI provider, plugins, notifications and data |
