The Dashboard tab shows a set of widgets, such as your Campus Card balance, network fee and the
teaching week. You can have the assistant build and change widgets, or add the widgets that
plugins provide. This page explains how.

## What's on the page

A greeting sits at the top. Once you are signed in and have a timetable, it also shows the teaching
week, the class in progress or the next one, and how many classes are left today.

The widgets come below the greeting. The first time you open the app, the dashboard has three:
**Today** (the date and teaching week), **Reminders** (how many reminders are pending) and
**Teaching week**. You can remove or replace them at any time.

Widget data refreshes on its own. When you open the dashboard or come back to the app, outdated
data is fetched again, and while the dashboard is on screen each widget refreshes at its own
interval. Pull down on the page to refresh every widget now.

## Add a widget

There are two ways.

**Ask the assistant in chat.** Tell it what you want to see, for example "Put my Campus Card
balance at the top of the dashboard" or "Add a card showing this semester's teaching week". If a
plugin already provides a suitable widget, the assistant uses it. Otherwise it builds one from a
plugin's read-only tools. To change what a widget shows, also just tell the assistant in chat.

**Add a plugin widget from the gallery.**

1. Tap **+** (**Add widget**) at the top right of the dashboard. When the dashboard is empty, tap
   **Add a plugin widget**.
2. The gallery lists the widgets that come with the plugins you have turned on. Tap **Add** next to
   one.

Plugin widgets include **Campus Card balance**, **Network fee**, **Canteen crowds**,
**Free classrooms**, **Latest notices** and **Authenticator**. To see which widgets a plugin
provides, open its page under **Settings → Plugins** and look at **Dashboard widgets**.

When the dashboard is empty, a few examples are listed below, such as "Show my next three
reminders". Tapping one switches to the Chat tab and sends it to the assistant. The
"+ Ask the AI in chat to add or change widgets" box at the bottom of the dashboard opens the Chat
tab.

## Widget sizes

Each widget has one of three sizes.

| Size | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Small | Half the screen wide; good for a single number |
| Wide | The full width; its height follows the content |
| Large | The full width and taller; for the most important information |

## Edit the dashboard

1. Long-press any widget, or tap the edit button (**Edit dashboard**) at the top right.
2. Drag the handle on the left of a row up or down to reorder.
3. Tap the size label on the right of a row to switch between **Small**, **Wide** and **Large**.
4. Tap the trash button to remove a widget. There is no second confirmation.
5. Tap **Done**.

You can also ask the assistant in chat to reorder, resize or remove widgets.

If a widget shows "Couldn't refresh", tap **Retry** to fetch its data again. If the plugin behind a
widget is turned off, the widget says it is off or unavailable on this device.
